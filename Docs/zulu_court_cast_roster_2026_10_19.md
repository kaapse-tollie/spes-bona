The original added opening adults receive the politician role so their AF or LO membership does not depend on becoming an IG leader. Langazana's Devout appointment supplies the native politician role; Sigananda enters as a politician without replacing the current RF leader. Mkabayi remains LO leader and Ndlela AF leader. No new army or general office is fabricated. Military service motivates AF assignments; these are game interpretations, not claims that nineteenth-century chiefs belonged to Victoria 3 interest groups. The [28 October leadership brief](zulu_ig_leadership_research_2026_10_28.md) records the two additions' evidence and implementation defaults.

Following 1 belongs to the incumbent office. Non-ruling eligible adult princes have following 0 unless a named shared house is already recorded: each heads their own branch and contributes native prominence, including the +20 claimant modifier (25 October tuning), to their coalition. Mkabayi, Ndlela and Zulu/Komfiya retain their named houses. Shared houses sum eligible adult members once and use their leader's commitments; select the leader before SB's −10 subordinate penalty. A replacement leader retains the following ID, but not their predecessor's promises.

All adult non-leaders, including unaffiliated advisers and newly registered procedural councillors, receive −10 Prominence. Unaffiliated councillors contribute individually and appear outside Great Houses. Generated surnames do not establish historical house membership. No additional characters or kinship claims are introduced by this presentation change. The opening succession is unacknowledged: Mpande remains alive and politically available but is not designated heir.

No new personality traits were inferred from command or office. The earlier interpretive Mnyamana/Hamu traits are removed in this batch. Existing broad political ideologies and allegiance cards remain separate. Oral testimony is labelled as historical evidence, never treated as interchangeable with authored fiction. Exact new birthdays and undocumented commitments remain unspecified; the approximate ages above only ensure a plausible adult opening cast.

## 1. One-state origin after Klip River

ZUL owns only Zululand. Test 50:50 and 75:25 backing, backing either prince. The native split may create two country-owned state objects in the same region. Record native barracks, conscripts, servicemen/officers and battalion manpower. There must be **no Wartime Muster building, no conscription suspension and no scripted relocation/rebuilding**. Verify both winners and the usual character/Firearms/Prerogative outcomes.

## 2. Multi-state origin: Zululand and Zulu-owned Natal

Test 50:50, 60:40 and 75:25 quotes, both player choices. The stronger side retains the mainland preference. Save immediately before the choice, after recruitment preparation, immediately after the split, and four/eight weeks later.

Record, per side: regular and muster levels; selected training methods; native conscript capacity/units; serviceman/officer workforce and dependants; vacancies and officer qualifications; available battalions, actual manpower, organisation, supply and treasury.

Check the pre-split configured levy pool survives the small state's capacity change, subject to workforce limits. The customary muster should retain 99:1 professions. Native hiring and training must produce usable troops. Repeat to distinguish coalition effects from battle randomness.

Both factions must show **Wartime Provisions** (+150 percentage points of Food Security). Record Food Security and starving population after the first native update and four/eight weeks later; a displayed pre-relief zero does not reveal its raw negative score. Food prices need not fall: this trial adds relief rather than market supply. One-state splits and unrelated countries must have no such modifier.

## 3. Duplicate-conscription and AI check

On both multi-state factions, attempt to add/raise ordinary conscripts; let the AI run too. No additional usable levy pool may reappear while Wartime Muster is active, including after reload or a native conscription bonus. The −1,000% displayed restriction is a clamped zero-rate gate, not negative capacity. Verify no invalid/negative centre levels. Check the AI actually mobilises and uses the custom muster's units. Failure blocks this prototype.

## 4. More than two states and scarce recruits

Use uneven military infrastructure and civilian population. Recruitment stays in each actual country's selected state; internal surplus is used before cross-border transfers. NAL/foreign owners of other portions of the same region remain untouched. Low eligible workforce reduces temporary levy capacity while retaining existing regulars. No slaves or employed non-peasant civilians are moved. Check officers independently: a civilian reserve does not guarantee officer qualifications.

## 5. Both winners, merging and demobilisation

Test either side winning, including a later native state merge. Build an extra regular barracks during the war and record casualties. All temporary muster buildings, conscription restrictions and Wartime Provisions disappear once; later regular construction remains. The authored barracks relocation is reversed to its recorded original regional distribution; affected donor units may lose manpower/experience through native rebuilding. Ordinary native conscription becomes available again. Surviving people return to the recorded pre-muster Natal/Zululand ratio; casualties are not recreated. Check both counts and actual employment. Check Food Security after the reunited market updates too.

## 6. Cancellation, duplication and a later war

Cancel before the split: no post-split muster or civilian transfer. Pre-creation regular barracks relocation is reversed when its original receipt exists. No population is moved on cancellation. Repeat callbacks/save-reload during a live war: no further redistribution. Test cancellation after an actual split if the controller reaches that path. A subsequent war must classify the current origin afresh—especially a multi-state first war followed by a one-state war after Klip River.

Release criterion: the multi-state path yields usable forces on both sides and a repeatable relationship between backing and strength, without duplicating conscripts; the one-state path retains native division. Recruitment and battle balance are not established by passing static tests.

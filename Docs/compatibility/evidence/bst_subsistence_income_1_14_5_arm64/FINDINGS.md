# Output-only Mountain Refuge: native findings (1 November 2026)

Read-only investigation. No gameplay changes or engine launch. The tested modifier has `building_subsistence_output_mult = 0.30`; the user's throughput line remains commented out.

## Executable and reproduction

`manifest.json` records the exact executable, whole-file SHA-256, 18 symbol names and complete llvm-objdump argument arrays. Outputs are arm64 disassembly. Executable SHA-256: `e4806a9b315c9ed40bdafd5e6a9e3e46aca741a0a0890bded906ebcc295e2a13`. ## Verified findings

- `CalcBaseYearlySubsistenceOutput` applies output add and multiplier; Serfdom's base 3 with +0.30 becomes 3.9 per worker/year. Weekly base divides by 52; `CalcSubsistenceOutput(Pop)` multiplies by workforce, not dependants. See `base_yearly_subsistence.txt`, `base_subsistence.txt`, `pop_subsistence.txt`. Fixed-point truncation matters: old 3.3/52 becomes .06346; 2,702 workers therefore gave exactly 171.46892. New 3.9/52 is .075; 2,794 workers give exactly 209.55. This matches the new save, proving the bonus is active. Relative to the former +10% modifier, +30% is an 18.18% increase in this income.
- Dependant demand and income are separate: `ComputeScaledConsumptionFactorForBuyPackagePrice` scales household basket by workforce plus dependants times `DEPENDENT_CONSUMPTION_RATIO` (installed value .5). Dependants receive no subsistence-output payment in the function above, but do receive separate dependent wages (`dependent_wages.txt`). Their positive saved dependent-income entry must be retained when calculating the household budget.
- Native budget category 0 is wage income and 1 is subsistence income (`pay_wages.txt`, 0x10062b94c..978); 2 is dependent income (`process_dependent_wages.txt`, 0x10111edf8..ee08). Category 7 is needs/purchases: the purchases task negates `UpdateWealth`'s returned consumption amount and writes category 7 (0x100e19c30..c40). Category 10 is the grouped land/per-capita tax bucket: the wage task writes negative poll tax to 10 (0x10062b8cc..8dc), and its filtered display computes land, heathen and per-capita taxes (`weekly_budget_categories.txt`, 0x1010eda88..ab8). Do not label it exclusively land tax. Category 12 is consumption tax (purchases task 0x100e19c60..c70).
- The new peasant budget has 44.06968 wage income +209.55 subsistence +119.67696 dependent income -323.00165 purchases -37.60724 grouped tax = +12.68775 weekly net. Wealth is 2 while Food Security is .07565. Positive money flow and severe starvation coexist in the actual save.
- `NFoodSecurity::CalcFoodSecurityData(Pop)` computes `clamp(1 + state_food_security_modifier - basic_food_cost_share - basic_food_shortage_severity, 0, 1)`; see `pop_food_security.txt` (0x100668058..130). The first penalty uses the wealth-level household basket and local basic-food prices (`basic_food_cost_share.txt`, `basic_food_cache.txt`). The second call is exactly `NMarketUtils::CalcShortageSeverity(CPopNeed const&, CState const&, CString*)` at 0x10066808c: it receives need and state, no Pop or peasant consumption multiplier. It is a common state/need shortage penalty; the peasant market-consumption reduction is not a direct exemption at this call. Extra in-kind income is not credited as direct food coverage in this formula. Income can affect wealth and therefore the basket indirectly.

## Limits

These formulas explain why the in-kind bonus can improve money without resolving Food Security; they do not establish the exact cost-share/shortage decomposition for this saved Pop. No exact shortage fraction is claimed. The low workforce share (2,794 of 21,973 = 12.7156%, versus the installed normal reference .25) reduces subsistence income per household member, but this investigation does not establish its historical cause. The common shortage treatment is an engine abstraction, not a confirmed engine defect. Existing Serfdom hiring protection remains a separate previously documented production bottleneck; this income-only change removes neither that protection nor physical grain shortage.

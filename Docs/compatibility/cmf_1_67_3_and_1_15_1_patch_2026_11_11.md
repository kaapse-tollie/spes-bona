## Game delta and SB ports

including one new directory, with no removals. Every path has a disposition in
the [depot review](evidence/1_15_1_implementation/current_target.json).

each reconstruction matches the old Steam SHA-1. These cover the four movement
files, Armed Forces, colonial claims and regional copper. This establishes
complete-file parity outside those changes.
[Reconstruction proofs](evidence/1_15_1_implementation/native_delta_proofs.json)

SB changes five gameplay files:

- Chiefdoms labour movement: one character-ideology correction; restrictions retained.
- Cultural-minority movement: native secession flag; NAL/Zulu handoff, CMF hooks
  and predicate optimisations retained. SB's Natal civil-rights movement remains
  unable to revolt or secede.
- Armed Forces: native ownership attraction for employed upper strata, twice
  workplace military-industry assets capped at 150. SB's aristocrat modifier,
  cheap guards, wage branches and government checks remain.
- Colonial claims: exact native `infamy_value`, including the temporary
  `infamy_ignores_claim` scope and decentralised discount. The old manual formula
  and cap disappear with that block; the Boer-region exclusion and every other
  native action field remain.
- Regional map: only copper capacities change to Namaqualand 9, Zambia 21 and
  Katanga 35, totalling 65. Other SB resources, eighteen authored state blocks,
  geometry, cold values, repaired splines and travel network remain.

PHL/WBL deliberately retain SB tier 6 plus `international_trade`, `rationalism`
and `tech_bureaucracy`, laws, institutions and omission of the native Highveld
journal. Native tier 4 is not imported. Copied culture, flag and coat-of-arms
objects are unchanged; source-file pins are refreshed. Non-shadowed AI changes
are inherited after caller review. Reviewed dominion localisation and markers
are unchanged; only native collision evidence is refreshed.

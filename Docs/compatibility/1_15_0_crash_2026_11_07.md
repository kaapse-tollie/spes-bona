# Victoria 3 1.15.0 — inherited-law journal crash, 7 November 2026

**CRASH115-01: source correction applied; engine replay pending.** The crash
stack shows a journal being activated inside a directly inherited law's
`on_activate`, before rebel-country creation finishes. SB's Chiefly Council
callback is the strongly supported source: it bypassed the previously guarded
law on-action and called institutional reconciliation too early. The saved
campaign has that law and an active Zulu succession crisis.

## Failure mechanism

The exception follows this native path:

```text
UpdateCivilWars → TriggerRevolution → CreateCivilWarCountry
→ CreateNonDynamicCivilWarCountry → CreateCountry → InheritLawsFrom
→ ActivateCountryLaw → LawType::ExecuteOnActivate
→ scripted effect → three nested ifs → add_journal_entry
→ JournalEntry::OnActivated +356
```

Read-only inspection of the installed **1.15.0 ARM64 binary** confirms that
`CreateCountry` calls law inheritance before land transfer and
`OnCreateCountry`; the latter creates the country's AI. The journal activation
offset is at the AI access/write sequence. This repeats the lifecycle hazard
identified in the [1.14.5 investigation](zulu_contested_crown_2026_10_27.md#follow-up-rebel-country-creation-crash--guarded-replay-pending),
through a different entry path.

The [two custom constitutional laws](../../common/laws/05_sb_chiefly_constitution.txt)
invoke `sb_institutions_reconcile` directly. The existing
[`sb_on_institutions_law` guard](../../common/on_actions/sb_institutions_on_actions.txt)
only protects the separate `on_law_activated` route. Previously, the shared
helper could initialise a court and add `je_sb_zulu_kingdom` even when the
country had no land or AI. Its three nested conditions match the stack.

The stack establishes the operation and native creation phase. It contains
neither the actual JE identifier nor country ID, and the crash has no core or
register snapshot. Attribution to ZUL's Chiefly Council succession is therefore
a strong source/save inference, not a directly recorded actor. Nearby naval HQ,
tooltip and agitator scope warnings, and low reported available memory, are
not established causes of this stack; they received no speculative changes.

## Correction

The [shared reconciliation entry](../../common/scripted_effects/sb_institutions_effects.txt)
now requires both the existing post-lobby marker and `is_country_alive = yes`
on its current country, before any institutional effects. This covers direct
law callbacks as well as on-actions and preserves foreign-root routing.
Readiness here prevents the observed **pre-land inheritance phase**; it is not
a claim that owning land proves every native subsystem is ready in all callbacks.

The existing post-history bootstrap and completed rebel-country handoff still
reconcile the court. No new timer, flag, law change or succession redesign was
added. Native party/voting cleanup remains unchanged. Ready countries retain
the same eligibility, movements, modifiers and persistent journal behaviour.

The new [source-executed regression](../../tests/test_zulu_institutions_core.py)
runs both actual law activation bodies and the separate Law-root on-action for
ZUL and an inherited-profile rebel, with a foreign GBR root. It covers
post-lobby/pre-land creation, pre-history live countries and both prerequisites
absent, followed by repeated ready-country reconciliation and callbacks.

Before the correction, all **12 subcases failed**: eight attempted journal
activation without an AI and four mutated institutions before history finished.
Afterwards all pass, including one history-authored journal, one set of three
movements, completed-country activation and unchanged unrelated countries.
All **66 focused institutional, law and court tests pass**. The fixture executes
parsed SB source; native party cleanup and some court helpers are traced rather
than simulated. It does not execute the engine or prove native crash prevention.

Full target-aware validation ran **1,103 tests: 1,094 passed, eight skipped and
one pre-existing localisation-review failure**. Its duplicate localisation gate
also fails, leaving 15/17 validator rows passed, warned or skipped. The unchanged

drift in Klip River `.060`/`.080` and Natal `.050`. No localisation or review
baseline was edited. Dependency integrity, overrides, maps/naval connectivity,
script contracts and lifecycle checks pass. Tiger 1.19.0 remains unsuitable as

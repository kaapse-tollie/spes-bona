## Levy recruitment

In separate runs, declare a comparable war, mobilise regulars and raise the available conscripts for Basutoland, Zululand, Gaza and Swaziland. Include Nguni after formation and an eligible Basotho AI frontier conflict that requests extra battalions. Use an unprotected labour-market control and a protected, incorporated subsistence state under the same military law. At declaration and weeks **1, 4 and 12**, record centre levels, ordered and raised units, current manpower, soldier and officer hires, qualifications, training, local vacancies and peasant employment. Keep state, law, supply and funding comparable. Log whether a unit failed to form, formed empty, or filled slowly.

| Country | Standing manpower in archived saves | Authored reserves | Observed unraised reserves | Full strength using observed reserves | Peasants employed at the centre’s subsistence farms, Feb / May / Jun |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Basutoland (BST) | 1,000 | 6 | 3 | 4,000 | 2 / 14 / 18 |
| Zululand (ZUL) | 4,000 | 20 | 19 | 23,000 | 45,890 / 46,022 / 46,067 |
| Gaza (GZA) | 4,000 | 14 | 14 | 18,000 | 140,000 / 140,000 / 140,000 |
| Swaziland (SWZ) | 1,000 | 4 | 3 | 4,000 | 0 / 3 / 4 |

These are three 1.15.0 Cape campaign snapshots on 1 February, 1 May and 1 June 1836. The 39 reserve units had zero manpower at each snapshot. Filling those units would give **49,000** total including existing regulars. Filling all 44 authored reserve levels would give **54,000**. At 1,000 manpower the customary production method’s 99:1 ratio calls for 990 soldiers and 10 officers. The peasant-only modifier describes eligible infantry unit types. Recruitment also depends on workers available to change profession, qualifications, wages and training. The small Basotho and Swazi local workforces make particularly useful controls. [Save identities and per-country figures](evidence/1_15_2_implementation/levy_baseline.json)

At observed reserve strength, Zululand would need 18,810 soldiers and 190 officers, Gaza 13,860 and 140, and Basutoland and Swaziland 2,970 and 30 each. Using the 50% incorporated-subsistence floor under Serfdom, about 21,000 peasant workers in the Zululand recruiting state and 70,000 in Gaza were above half nominal capacity in February. These estimates count labour at its existing workplace. Officer recruitment, transfers and the net change in raised manpower need direct observation. The Basotho AI wartime helper can request 14 conscription levels. Record how many it actually raises. [Earlier Basotho mobilisation investigation](../zulu_playtest_followup_2026_10_30.md)

## Prerogative title — PLAY115-04

The type-level JE title is now the context-free **Prerogative**, safe in the lobby
and in-game previews that lack a `JournalEntry`. The existing
[live readout](../../localization/english/sb_institutions_l_english.yml) displays
**Royal Prerogative** or **Chiefly Prerogative** using its actual journal owner.
It does not use the player or an unrelated event root. Arithmetic, bar rendering
and widget layout are unchanged.

The [title regression](../../tests/test_zulu_institutions_core.py) now requires a
context-free public title and checks both owner-specific names, including a
foreign caller with the opposite constitution. This does not emulate GUI data
context propagation; type hover, tag switching and live rendering still need DP.
All affected text retains its existing TO REVIEW status; the
[review queue](../localisation_review_queue.md) records the changed live readout
and new diplomacy tooltip. Reviewed prose and approval baselines are untouched.

## Natal occupation timers — PLAY115-05

Both [custom Natal goals](../../common/war_goal_types/sb_story_war_goals.txt) now
omit `assent_required` and declare `mirrored_wargoal = { method = no_mirror }`.
Their existing Natal objectives, opposite participants and control conditions
remain intact. They use the native default **5 points per week** fill/depletion:
20 weeks from empty under continuous objective control. Losing control before
completion drains progress at the native rate.

`no_mirror` self-enforces without creating a reverse goal. These custom goals
make no immediate territorial transfer. Their
[recorder](../../common/scripted_effects/sb_story_war_effects.txt) retains the
accepted demand when it enforces, including by timer, and preserves the sovereign
at that point. Final story settlement still waits for the authenticated peace or
existing deleted-play recovery path. Repeated enforcement is idempotent; both
accepted demands still mean mutual stand-down. The other assent-only story
humiliation routes continue to reject timer callbacks. No force-peace controller
has been reintroduced.

This follows the pinned
[native war-goal contract](</Users/depro/Library/Application Support/Steam/steamapps/common/Victoria 3/game/common/war_goal_types/war_goal_types.md>) and
[separate timer/final callbacks](</Users/depro/Library/Application Support/Steam/steamapps/common/Victoria 3/game/common/on_actions/00_code_on_actions.txt>).
The [source tests](../../tests/test_blood_river_outcome_events.py) cover both
recipients, repeated receipts, no early settlement/recovery while the play
exists, final resolution once and unrelated-play rejection. They do not establish
native bar progression, callback ordering, AI peace acceptance or save/load.

## Natalia joining Swaziland — PLAY115-06

A [keyed side-joining rule](../../common/scripted_rules/zz_sb_swazi_join_rules.txt)
retains the complete native 1.15 eligibility body and adds one
[guard](../../common/scripted_triggers/sb_swazi_triggers.txt): Natalia cannot join
SWZ against ZUL when the documented side/initiator scopes match the live authored
play saved on ZUL. It checks the live play as well as the markers; a stale record,
unrelated conflict, other joining country or opposite side is not barred.
The rule does not assume an undocumented `scope:diplomatic_play` context.

The source fixture verifies native-body parity, the marked conflict, foreign
roots and negative controls. The added object is recorded in the
[override inventory](override_inventory.json): 41 same-path files and 125 keyed
overrides. Manual joining, AI/sway routing and applicable automatic treaty/subject
participation need engine checks. This does not eject an existing participant or
migrate the running campaign.

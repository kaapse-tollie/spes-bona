## Live rules

- Static caps implement the final 17-state design.
- Eight regional deposits hold 95 hidden diamond levels. A territorial owner's
  Dynamite, Pumpjacks or Pneumatic Tools opens the region's annual surveys. A
  successful report adds capacity for Diamond Mines. Kimberley's event provides
  its separate 20 levels.
- Iron and coal additions remain immediately usable and post one-time notifications.
- The remaining scripted gates retain technology-acquisition, game-start, and
  state-owner-change hooks. The eight legacy gold/oil stages are unchanged: native
  inspection found that their capacity effect does not add undiscovered reserves.
  That separate issue is recorded in the open audit register.
- Natal coffee, Zululand cotton, and Lourenço Marques cotton remain deliberate crop
  abstractions. Griqualand West and Bechuanaland receive the two approved one-grain
  corrections.
- Drakensberg 8 and Namaqualand 5 are conservative arable playtesting values. A fresh
  start must verify Drakensberg population viability and whether split-state rounding
  actually gives Cape-owned Walvis Bay an arable level.
- A fresh campaign is required for validation because state-region data is static.

Configured-horizon totals are **383 arable, 65 wood, 149 coal, 33 fishing, 46 iron,
20 lead, 1 sulfur, 17 whaling, 92 gold potential, 115 diamonds, 32 rubber, and 6 oil
potential**.

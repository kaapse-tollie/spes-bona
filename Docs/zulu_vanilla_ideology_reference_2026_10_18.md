# ZUL ideology reference — Vanilla and proposed ideologies

**Proposal superseded, 18 October:** DP has dropped the PB/LO faction split and
the proposed broad packages below. They remain a record of the earlier proposal;
the Vanilla cards remain a reference. See the
[crown-and-chiefs exploration](zulu_crown_and_chiefs_exploration_2026_10_18.md)
for the new narrow-movement alternatives.

One support table per ideology. The [effect matrix](zulu_ideology_effect_matrix_2026_09_20.md) retains the comparison by law group; this card shows what each ideology supports on its own.

Vanilla baseline: installed 1.14.5, Steam build `25629520`, read on 18 October 2026. Vanilla descriptions preserve the English game text, with only `#lore`/`#!` display markup removed. New ideologies have a design-purpose note, not invented Vanilla description text. This is design documentation only.

`++` strongly approves; `+` approves; `0` explicitly neutral; `-` disapproves; `--` strongly disapproves; **N/A** no position. Each table lists that ideology’s positions before leaders or other ideologies contribute. Proposed law variants are labelled.

**Earlier proposal status:** these tables preserve the superseded matrix without changing ratings. At that stage, bureaucracy, policing, economics, land and related changes had been reopened for review. Royal Prerogative’s Appointed Bureaucrats `+` was the challenged proposal; keeping Vanilla `0` was a recommendation, not an approved replacement. The former new army opinions concerned only Amabutho and Chiefly Militias.

## Why the disputed changes were proposed

These are the rationale and limits of the earlier proposal, not assertions that the changes are historically necessary or approved.

| Area | Earlier proposal and reasoning | What needs reconsideration |
|---|---|---|
| LO bureaucracy | Appointed Bureaucrats `0 → +` to represent appointments controlled by a royal centre; Hereditary `+` and Elected `-` stayed as they were | An optional inference about administration, not a requirement for monarchy or a sourced finding about ZUL. Retaining Vanilla neutral is the more conservative recommendation pending a specific bureaucracy model |
| PB bureaucracy | Hereditary `- → +`, Elected `+ → -`, Appointed stays `0`; replace the elected/meritocratic orientation with hereditary chiefly office | This addresses a concrete mismatch if PB represents hereditary chiefs, but elected local government is not intrinsically incompatible with all later notables. Keep the exact transition open |
| Policing | LO numerical stances unchanged. PB Local Police `0 → +`, Dedicated `+ → 0`, Militarized `++ → 0`, to represent local chiefly authority | Plausible institutional inference, not part of fixing uSuthu activism. Removing Patriotic also drops internal-security and navy positions; those losses must be deliberate |
| Economics | Both groups share Traditionalism/Agrarianism approval; PB stops treating them as commercial obstacles and LO loses much of its wider conservative economic programme | Replacing the urban-commercial model is relevant to chiefly PB, but weakening LO Traditionalism and neutralizing opposition to modern economic systems was an additional design choice. It is not forced by renaming the IGs |
| Land reform | Remove Hierarchic land positions instead of equating customary tenure with Serfdom/private freehold/collectivization | Avoiding a false equivalence is a modelling concern, not evidence that royals/chiefs have no land interests. Complete removal also removes resistance to peasant proprietorship and collectivization; this needs a positive land-policy model |
| Tax, welfare, trade and other policies | Shared Homestead Order selected land-based tribute and omitted many wider generic positions | Package replacement made these disappear as well. Each must be retained or changed consciously; none is required merely to narrow the restorationist movement |
| Army | Previous proposal added preferences for ordinary army laws as well as the two SB laws | DP now prefers new army positions only for Amabutho and Chiefly Militias. Updated proposed columns accordingly; original Vanilla stances remain recorded |

## Current discussion status

- **Agreed direction:** new army-specific positions only for Amabutho and Chiefly Militias. Other ordinary army-law rows are N/A in the proposed packages; this does not alter their original Vanilla columns.
- **Reopened:** bureaucracy, policing, economic, land and other broad IG changes. Their existing proposed ratings are under review, not approved or implemented.
- **Recommendation for the next revision:** use selective variants where a concrete chiefly/royal institutional conflict calls for a changed stance. Do not treat wholesale removal of unrelated positions as necessary. The separate uSuthu core can still be narrow even when its recruiting IGs have wider interests.
- **No runtime changes:** only the design matrix/record and this baseline reference are updated.
